DATA SOURCE SUMMARY
-------------------

SOURCE A: FRED Economic Data
  Endpoint: https://api.stlouisfed.org/fred/series/observations
  Series Count: 7 (expanded from 4)
  Priority: HIGH

  Series:
  - WPU1017: Steel Mill Products PPI
  - PCU331331: Primary Metal Manufacturing PPI
  - TRUCKD11: Truck Tonnage Index
  - RAILFRTCARLOADSD11: Rail Freight Carloads
  - TSIFRGHT: Freight Transportation Services Index (NEW)
  - IPMAN: Industrial Production - Manufacturing (NEW)
  - INDPRO: Industrial Production Index (NEW)

SOURCE B: Census VIP - Construction Spending
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/vip
  Priority: HIGH
  Update: Monthly (1st working day, +2 months lag)

  Focus: Private non-residential, public construction
  Use: Construction demand for steel products

SOURCE C: Census M3 - Manufacturers' Shipments/Orders
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/m3
  Priority: HIGH
  Update: Monthly (~23 working days)

  NAICS Categories:
  - 331: Primary Metal Manufacturing
  - 332: Fabricated Metal Products
  - 333: Machinery Manufacturing
  - 336: Transportation Equipment

  Data Types: SM (Shipments), NO (New Orders), UO (Unfilled Orders), TI (Inventories)

SOURCE D: Census ADVM3 - Advance Durable Goods (NEW)
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/advm3
  Priority: HIGH
  Update: Monthly (~18 working days, 5 days before M3)

  Advantage: Leading indicator released early
  Use: Quick-turn demand forecasting

SOURCE E: Census MRTS - Monthly Retail Trade (NEW)
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/mrts
  Priority: MEDIUM
  Update: Monthly (~21 working days)

  Categories:
  - 441: Motor Vehicle & Parts Dealers (auto steel proxy)
  - 444: Building Material & Garden Equipment
  - 4441: Building Material & Supplies

  Use: Consumer demand for steel-intensive products

SOURCE F: Census RES - New Residential Construction (NEW)
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/resconst
  Priority: MEDIUM-HIGH
  Update: Monthly (~17th)

  Metrics: Housing starts, building permits, completions
  Use: Residential steel demand (rebar, structural steel)

SOURCE G: Census MARTS - Advance Retail Sales
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/marts
  Priority: MEDIUM
  Status: Available for implementation

  Use: Early consumer demand signals

SOURCE H: Census MTIS - Manufacturing & Trade Inventories
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/mtis
  Priority: MEDIUM
  Status: Available for implementation

  Use: Supply chain inventory tracking, I/S ratios

SOURCE I: Census NHS - New Home Sales
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/nhs
  Priority: MEDIUM
  Status: Available for implementation

  Use: Housing market health, construction demand

SOURCE J: Census International Trade
  Endpoint: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/intltrade/imports/hs
  Priority: MEDIUM
  Status: Manual/API hybrid

  HS Codes: 7206-7229 (iron, steel products)
  Use: Import competition, trade policy impact

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
-------------------------

Standard Census EITS Query Pattern:
  URL: https://api.census.gov/data/timeseries/eits/{dataset}
  Parameters:
    - get: cell_value,time_slot_id,category_code,data_type_code,seasonally_adj
    - for: 'US' or '' (varies by dataset)
    - time: from+YYYY-MM
    - key: {api_key}

Data Format:
  - JSON (two-dimensional array)
  - First row: column headers
  - Subsequent rows: data values

Retry Logic:
  - 3 attempts with exponential backoff
  - Timeout: 30 seconds per request

ANALYTICS FRAMEWORK
-------------------

Composite Indicators:
  1. Steel Demand Index
     = 0.30 × M3 New Orders (Primary Metals)
     + 0.25 × Construction Spending (Non-res)
     + 0.20 × Durable Goods New Orders
     + 0.15 × Housing Starts
     + 0.10 × Auto Sales

  2. Supply Chain Pressure Index
     = Inventory/Sales Ratio × Freight Cost × Unfilled Orders Change

Leading Indicators (3-6 months):
  - New orders (M3, ADVM3)
  - Building permits (RES)
  - New home sales (NHS)

Coincident Indicators:
  - Shipments (M3)
  - Housing starts (RES)
  - Retail sales (MRTS)

Lagging Indicators:
  - Inventories (MTIS)
  - Completions (RES)
  - Unfilled orders (M3)
