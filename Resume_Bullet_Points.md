2. **Show Impact**: Focus on business outcomes and decisions enabled by your analysis
3. **Use Action Verbs**: Developed, Analyzed, Built, Designed, Implemented, Optimized, Created

5. **Balance Technical & Business**: Show you can do the work AND communicate value
