# Personal Story - SHARE Model Business Interview Version

## Matthew Tonks - Data Consulting Success Story

---

### **SITUATION - Setting the Context**

Early in my career at a consulting firm, I was assigned to my first client project after completing two months of training on Informatica data pipeline tools.

*(pause)*

The engagement involved implementing a framework to process multiple EDI file formats for a critical business system. The team was minimal - just a project manager, a QA worker, and myself. The previous developer had been removed for poor performance, setting high expectations for delivery.

---

### **HIGHLIGHT - The Key Challenge**

The challenge was significant: the resume being sent to the client described someone with years of high-level experience, but I had barely two months of training on technology I didn't even know existed before joining.

*(pause)*

Project constraints made everything worse. We were limited to 40-hour work weeks, but inefficient meetings consumed most of our time - daily standups took an hour, weekly planning ran longer, plus mandatory knowledge-sharing sessions. We had only 24 productive hours out of 40.

When deadlines hit, we had a completely unusable demo just one week before presentation.

---

### **ACTION - What I Did**

Instead of continuing 16-hour days, I made three strategic decisions:

**First,** I took complete ownership of all technical aspects, ensuring everything was bulletproof for deployment.

**Second,** I started communicating directly with the client's team - no more hiding behind project management layers. I talked to their developers, hosted knowledge transfer sessions, and gathered actionable feedback.

**Third,** I shifted focus from just technical requirements to understanding their actual business needs.

---

### **RESULT - What Happened**

The breakthrough came when the client realized the major improvements our framework would provide and how completely we covered their file processing needs.

*(pause)*

The demo exceeded expectations. We not only met technical requirements but delivered clear business value. The client was impressed with both the solution and the direct communication approach.

---

### **EVALUATION/ENHANCEMENT - Key Learning**

This experience taught me that **success combines technical competence with business understanding and effective communication.** The more I succeed with technology, the less I need to rely on it alone.

*(pause)*

This foundation shaped my approach to every subsequent project and led me to data science, where combining technical skills with business insight is essential. I learned that pressure reveals what's truly important - in my case, adaptability and business focus are just as valuable as technical expertise.

---

## **Interview-Specific Adaptations:**

### **For Technical Roles:**
- Emphasize the Informatica tools and EDI processing complexity
- Highlight technical ownership and quality assurance approach
- Mention specific troubleshooting and system integration challenges

### **For Business/Consulting Roles:**
- Focus on client relationship management and communication breakthrough
- Emphasize business needs analysis over technical requirements
- Highlight the business value delivery and client satisfaction

### **For Leadership Positions:**
- Stress the ownership mindset and team dynamics navigation
- Emphasize strategic decision-making under pressure
- Highlight the ability to turn around failing projects

### **Follow-up Question Preparation:**

**"What would you do differently?"**
*"I'd establish direct client communication channels earlier and push for clearer expectations from the start."*

**"How do you handle pressure?"**
*"I focus on what I can control - in this case, taking ownership, improving communication, and understanding real business needs rather than just technical specs."*

**"Tell me about a time you had to learn quickly"**
*"This entire project - I had to master complex data integration tools while delivering production-ready code, but the key was knowing when to ask for help and focusing on business outcomes."*
