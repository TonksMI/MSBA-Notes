# When to use interactivity

## Why

Enhance engagement through exploration

Allow personalization and multidimensionality - User controls of filters views

supports progressive disclosure of info

## When to add interactivity

- Audiance and context miss alignment
  - when the goal is clarity and consistency not exploration
  - Executives investors or policy stakeholders often want answers not tools

- Cognitive and usability load
  - Too many filters hovers or moving parts create decision fatigue and attention fragmentation
  - Interaction should never make comprehension harder than the insight itself

- Technical or Governance Constraints
  - Complex interactions may break on mobile or edge devices or expose sensitive data
  - Can every intended user access and interpret this the same way

## Levels

### Level 1

Static

least complex

Best for executives and investors

No risks

### Level 2

Reactive hover highlight sort

2nd least Quick demos directors

Can hide key info

### Level 3

Selective

filters toggles dropdowns

analysts mid level managers

moderate cognitive load

### Level 4

Exploratory

Linked views drill downs

Data savvy users

High learning curve

## Dashboard interaction

Exploratory

find patterns used by analysts and managers

Explanatory

Tell stories

used by presenter and audience

## Design Guidelines

### Define the purpose before adding meaningful interactions 

limit to 1-3 actions

### Minimize cognitive load

Keep layouts clean and predictable

show complexity onlya when needed

### Design for inclusivity and performance

test across all platforms

slow dashboard erode trust

Guide the User's journey

### Establish logical flow

Provide a reset home button and persistent indicators of active filters

## Common Pitfalls

### Too much freedom Paradox of choice

Decision fatigue

Limit key actions add reset button

### Hidden Info Trap of ambiguity

Lost insight 

Confusion

Show activity filters

Surface key data

### Over engineered performanc and attention killers

slow and inaccessible dashboards

Optimize performance

Test all Platforms

### Misaligned Audience Classic Mistake

Message lost

Match design to audience needs
