## Uncertainty

Builds trust and credibility

- Transparency <> mean being weak showing uncertainty

- visual humility shows confidence and caution simultaneously

Prevents False Precision

- Sharp lines = False confidence

- Expectation management visualizng un certainty reframes stake holder expectations

Risk aware decision making

- Scenario thinking seeing multiple outcomes encourages contingency plans

- Bias Reduction Mitigates tendency to fixate on favorable single outcomes

Transparently Reflects Model limitations

- Ethical communication showing uncertainty respects your audiences right to informed representation

- Encourages refinement wide intervals sparks discussion about data quality feature selection or sample size

Contingency planning

- Decision flexibility adaptive planning

- systemic thinking anticipates variablility rather than reactive
